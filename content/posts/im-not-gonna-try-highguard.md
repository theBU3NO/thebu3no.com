highguard looks cool and neat and interesting and innovative. it looks {{< style word="smooth" type="green" >}}. i don't care if being the last game showcased at the game awards means something to you.

i'm not interested in a 3v3 competitive shooter. that's all there is to it.

well.. ok, that's all there *needs* to be to it but of course there's some bonus bull{{< style word="shit" type="brown" >}}.

i'm especially not interested in a 3v3 competitive **hero** shooter

i'm especially not interested in a 3v3 competitive hero shooter where i have to farm at the beginning of each match and pray to rngesus.

i'm like, {{< style word="super-duper" type="rainbow" >}} not interested in a 3v3 competitive hero shooter in a gigantic map with castles where all i can think the whole time is "damn i wish there were some more fuckin people here." 

i think i'm really just mad at myself

>Highguard is a PvP raid shooter where players will ride, fight, and raid as Wardens, arcane gunslingers sent to fight for control of a mythical continent.

i took what they gave me and i dared to dream. i foolishly imagined that you might want more than 3 guys to capture a {{< style word="magical" type="blue" >}} continent. i hopelessly fabricated a world where maybe like 16 folks could be involved in the battle with the 50 foot tall siege engine.

i say this a lot but i'm fully aware that 3v3 being uninteresting is a me problem. my preferences were shaped by tribes 2 and alterac valley and planetside. it just {{< style word="hurts" type="red" >}} when something goes from being ripped straight out of my dreams to just another next in the discovery queue.

i hope highguard is a good game and you enjoy it.
